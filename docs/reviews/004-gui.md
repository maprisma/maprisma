1. **High** `flutter_app/lib/map_smart_card.dart:195` (`_close`) Collapsing the card cancels what is running. `_close`
   is called by the collapse arrow, a click on the header and Esc in every step, and it calls `tasks.cancel` on
   `_activeTask`. In the running step that is the model download: a user who folds the card away, expecting it to go on
   in the background, silently loses a download of many GB. Only the Cancel button may stop it. Worse, `_close` raises
   `_runToken` but leaves `_step`, so polling ends and the card, opened again, shows a progress bar for ever, in the
   working step as well. Fix: in the running step folding only hides: no cancel, no new token, polling goes on, and
   the card shows the state when opened again. In the working step it may cancel (the answer is wanted by nobody), but
   then `_step` must go back to idle with the text kept. Tests: collapse and Esc during a run send no `tasks.cancel`, the
   run finishes, opening shows done; collapse during working leaves a usable prompt.
   > Antigravity: fixed in af89641
2. **Low** `flutter_app/lib/map_smart_card.dart:304,409,475` Polling every 50 ms, up to many minutes during a download,
   where the rest of the app uses 200 ms (`_awaitTask`). Each `tasks.status` also runs `jobs.sync()` in the core, and
   a test recording gets 20 lines a second. Use 200 ms.
   > Antigravity: fixed in 18da008
3. **Low** `flutter_app/lib/map_smart_card.dart` (`_buildClarify`) A choice chip sends at once and spends tokens, but
   this step does not show who answers or what it costs. Uwe's rule is that the user decides each time: repeat the
   cost line above the chips, using the line from the idle step.
   > Antigravity: fixed in 968256e

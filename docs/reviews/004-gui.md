   Omarchy popup, 1100 wide, Models column: `min(922, 818)` puts it at 818, so it jumps 104 board pixels onto the
   Providers column when opened, even when the whole map is zoomed out and the column is fully visible; panned or
   zoomed in, it can end up outside the view. This also breaks "the card does not jump". Fix: stay at
   `16 + col * 302` in the board and bring it into view through the transform if it really is cut off (a change of
   `_transform` only when the card lies outside the visible rect, which is `transform.toScene` of the viewport
   corners), or leave it where it is. Add a test with a scale other than 1.
   > Antigravity: fixed in 937249e
2. **Medium** `flutter_app/lib/map_editor.dart:709,739` The smart cards are placed in the `Stack` before the line
   handles (`for (final curve in layout.curves…`), so a line's handle lies above an open card. Where a line runs
   under the card, a click or drag on the card hits the handle and moves or reconnects that line. Fix: place the
   smart cards last in the `Stack`, plus a test that tapping the card where a handle lies does not reach the handle.
   > Antigravity: fixed in aba1157

3. Fixed: focus is asked for whenever the window appears, whatever made it appear.
4. Fixed: the helper can be changed on the error step.
5. Fixed: the menu has a width of 220 at the right of the header, the title with its icon is a button in the status step, reviews keep their own height at the top of the window.

4. **Low** `flutter_app/lib/map_smart_card.dart:1071-1126`, `1964-2015`, `2018-2075`; `flutter_app/lib/task_card.dart:171-187` Header flex sizing squishes the title and 220 px helper dropdown while leaving `Spacer()` blank, and `Align(alignment: Alignment.topCenter)` around the reviews is a no-op:

# Review: 013-terminal-overlay

## Round 1 (Antigravity, 2026-11-01, commit 8cf019d)

1. **Switching terminal tabs or clicking the footer's camera button leaves keyboard focus off `TerminalView`, and app-level shortcuts (`Ctrl+,`, `Ctrl++`/`-`/`0`, `F1`, `F5`) fire behind the open terminal overlay** (`flutter_app/lib/agent_terminal.dart:177-180`, `197-206`; `flutter_app/lib/main.dart:328-333`, `3953-3978`, `4024-4095`, `4166-4168`, `4499-4525`).
   - **Switching tabs in `TerminalTabs` loses terminal focus:** `TerminalView` (`agent_terminal.dart:177-180`) has `autofocus: true` but no `key` tied to `session` (nor a `FocusNode` requested on change). When the user clicks another tab in `TerminalTabs` (`main.dart:4166-4168`), focus moves to the clicked `ChoiceChip` (outside `OverlayWindow`), and `AgentTerminalView` updates the existing `TerminalViewState` in place (`didUpdateWidget`). Because `package:xterm`'s `TerminalViewState` only requests focus on `autofocus: true` in `initState`, the newly shown terminal does not receive keyboard focus until the user clicks inside the terminal canvas.
   - **Clicking the footer's camera button (`terminal-screenshot`) moves focus to the `IconButton`:** Clicking `IconButton(key: ValueKey('terminal-screenshot'))` (`agent_terminal.dart:200`) gives keyboard focus to the camera button. Subsequent keys no longer go to the terminal program until the user clicks back inside `TerminalView`: `Space`/`Enter` takes another screenshot, and `F1` or `F5` (which are no longer intercepted by `TerminalView`) bubble up to `MaterialApp`'s `actions` (`main.dart:3953-3972`).
   - **App shortcuts and header actions are not blocked while the terminal overlay is open:** Unlike `smartWindowsShown.value > 0` (`main.dart:333`), `_visibleTerminal != null` is not part of `_navigationBlocked` (`main.dart:328-333`) and is not checked by `_HelpIntent` (`main.dart:3955`), `_SettingsIntent` (`main.dart:3961`), `_RefreshIntent` (`main.dart:3969`), or `_ZoomIntent` (`main.dart:3975`), nor does the `ModalBarrier` in `OverlayWindow` (`main.dart:4502`) cover the `AppBar` (`main.dart:4024-4095`). Even while `TerminalView` *has* focus, `package:xterm` returns `KeyEventResult.ignored` for `Ctrl+,`, `Ctrl+=`/`Ctrl++`, `Ctrl+-`, and `Ctrl+0`, so pressing `Ctrl+,` inside a terminal closes the terminal overlay and opens *Settings*, and pressing `Ctrl++`/`Ctrl+-`/`Ctrl+0` zooms the dimmed map underneath the terminal. And whenever focus is on a button inside `AgentTerminalView` (e.g. after clicking the camera symbol or pressing `Tab`) or on a `TerminalTabs` chip, pressing `F1` opens *Help* (replacing the terminal overlay with the Help card) and `F5` refreshes the map.
   - **How to reproduce:**
     1. Open a terminal and press `Ctrl+,` (opens *Settings* and hides the terminal) or `Ctrl++` (zooms the map behind the terminal); or
     2. Click the camera button (`terminal-screenshot`) in the terminal's footer (or switch between two running terminal tabs via `TerminalTabs`), then type or press `F1` — keystrokes do not reach the terminal, and `F1` opens *Help*.

### Answers (Claude)
1. Fixed, three parts. The app's keys (F1, Ctrl+comma, F5, zoom) do nothing while a terminal window is open; the header's buttons still work. A new terminal asks for the keyboard itself, because `autofocus` left it with whatever had it, and a click on a tab hands it back. The camera symbol takes no focus. App test: 'an open terminal keeps the keyboard and the app its keys'.

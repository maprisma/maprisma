## Round 1 (Antigravity, 2026-11-01, commit acdf549)

Note on map lights: `MapLightLayer` (`flutter_app/lib/map_lights.dart:121, 181`) culls light marks inside `occluders` around every box (`layout.boxes.values.map((r) => r.inflate(radius))`), so leaving map lights undimmed does not draw over or hide anything on faded boxes.

## Round 2 (Antigravity, 2026-11-01, commit 9b27b5d)

1. **`tests/test_link_hints.py:104` — Unit test still asserts the old `"click one of Ollama's models"` hint wording and fails**
   - **What goes wrong:** `link_check.py:104-105` updated the no-model-chosen warning hint from `"{0} does not use a model of {1} yet: click {0} on the map, then click one of {1}'s models."` to `"{0} does not use a model of {1} yet: draw a line on the map from {0} to one of {1}'s models."`, but `CheckTest.test_no_model_chosen_and_no_service_say_how_to_go_on` in `tests/test_link_hints.py:104` still asserts `self.assertIn("click one of Ollama's models", hints[0]["text"])`.
   - **How to reproduce:** Run `python3 -m unittest tests.test_link_hints` — `test_no_model_chosen_and_no_service_say_how_to_go_on` fails with `AssertionError: "click one of Ollama's models" not found in "OpenCode does not use a model of Ollama yet: draw a line on the map from OpenCode to one of Ollama's models."`.

2. **`flutter_app/lib/main.dart:381-385, 424-426, 3773-3784, 4333-4341` — Just after downloading or pulling a model on `Providers and models`, returning to the map does not refresh `_graph`, so the new model box is not on the map to draw a line to**
   - **What goes wrong:** `link_check.py:99-100` (and `agent_status.py:663` / `packages/maprisma_core/lib/src/setup_checks.dart:410` via `add_model_step`) tells the user to open the local LLM's models (`Providers and models`), download a model, and `"Then draw a line on the map from {2} to the model."` In `_runTask` (`flutter_app/lib/main.dart:381-385`), `models.download` and `models.pull` force `width = TaskCardWidth.full`. When the task completes (`flutter_app/lib/main.dart:425`), `_refresh(refreshMap: _halfTaskMapVisible(width, resultFocus))` passes `refreshMap: false` (because `TaskHost.usesHalfCard` at `flutter_app/lib/task_card.dart:42` is `false` for `TaskCardWidth.full`), so `_refresh` (`flutter_app/lib/main.dart:3780-3784`) skips `_freshMap()` and does not reset `_nextMapScan`. (Likewise, `model.import` at `flutter_app/lib/providers_view.dart:196` runs while `_showProviders` is `true`, and when `resultFocus` has no matching nodes in `_graph` or `_mapAreaWidth < 900`, `_halfTaskMapVisible` is also `false`.) When the user closes the task result card (`_closeCard`, `flutter_app/lib/main.dart:285`) and clicks `Back` on `Providers and models` (`flutter_app/lib/main.dart:4333-4341`: `setState(() => _showProviders = false)`) or presses `Esc`/`Backspace` (`_goBack`, `flutter_app/lib/main.dart:2046-2055`), neither path refreshes `_graph` or resets `_nextMapScan`. As a result, just after a model is added, the map still holds the pre-download `_graph` without the new model node until a manual `Refresh` (`F5`) or the next background scan tick (or indefinitely when `map_scan_interval` is `0` / `"Manual"` or `'inventory'` is unchecked in `map_scan_targets`).
   - **How to reproduce:** Connect an agent (e.g. Pi) to a local LLM that has no models yet (or set Automatic map updates to `Manual` in Settings). Open `Providers and models`, click `Download model` on that local LLM, download/pull a model, click `Close` on the `Succeeded` card, and click `Back` to return to the map. Look at the `Models` column on the map to draw a line from the agent to the new model: the newly downloaded model box is not on the map until `Refresh` (`F5` / `AI Map`) is clicked (or until the background scan timer fires).

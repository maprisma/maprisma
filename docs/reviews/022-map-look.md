# Review: 022-map-look

## Round 1 (Antigravity, 2026-11-02, commit 0c9c909)

1. **High** `flutter_app/lib/map_editor.dart:536-541`, `flutter_app/lib/map_editor.dart:1147-1158` Selecting an unconnected box (or a box with only an `offer` line) and then turning on the card's `used_only` filter hides the selected box (`layout.boxes` omits it), but `layout.nodes` still contains it (`flutter_app/lib/map_layout.dart:201-204`). Because `_toggleUsedOnly` does not prune hidden boxes from `_selected` and `_buildEditor` checks `_selected.any(layout.nodes.containsKey)` and `seed.retainWhere(layout.nodes.containsKey)` instead of `layout.boxes.containsKey`, `dimming` stays `true` after the selected box vanishes: the entire map remains greyed out (`opacity: .3` / `alpha: .2`) with no selected box visible on screen, and any `offer` neighbour of the vanished box stays lit via `layout.allEdges`. Reproduce: click an unconnected box (e.g. an unused MCP server or agent) to select it, then click `Show only what is connected or pinned` (`card-used-only-<id>`) in that card's header.
2. **Medium** `flutter_app/lib/map_layout.dart:243`, `flutter_app/lib/map_editor.dart:1333` When `used_only` is enabled on an unfolded card that has no connected or pinned boxes (`ids` is empty), `height` is computed as `32 + ids.length * mapRowStep = 32.0`. The card's header widget (`flutter_app/lib/map_editor.dart:1329-1334`) has `height: 36` and a folded card has `height: 38.0`, so the painted card (`32.0` px) is shorter than its own header bar and buttons, and clicking **Collapse** on the empty card makes it grow from `32.0` px to `38.0` px. Reproduce: on a card with no connected or pinned boxes, click `Show only what is connected or pinned` in the card header.
3. **Medium** `flutter_app/lib/map_layout.dart:282-292`, `flutter_app/lib/map_editor.dart:1832-1840` An `agent-link` curve (an agent using another agent in column 1) starts and ends at `a.right = b.right = card.rect.right - 12` with control points at `side = card.rect.right + 10`, so its X coordinate is `card.rect.right - 12 + 66 * t * (1 - t)`. For `t` in `[0, 0.24]` and `[0.76, 1.0]` the curve lies inside `card.rect`, and at `t = 0.5` it extends at most `4.5` px past `card.rect.right`. When `card_border['on'] == true`, `_MapPaint` clips out `card.rect`, cutting off both ends of the `agent-link` arc inside the Agents card and leaving only a tiny detached sliver beside the middle of the card (almost completely covered when `border['width']` is 4–8 px) that never reaches either agent. Reproduce: turn on **Settings › Map › Borders around category cards** when one agent uses another (`activity.<agent>.uses`).
4. **Medium** `flutter_app/lib/map_lights.dart:121`, `flutter_app/lib/map_lights.dart:174-182`, `flutter_app/lib/map_editor.dart:1490-1499` When `card_border['on'] == true`, `_MapPaint` clips lines to the outside of `layout.cards.values`, but `MapLightLayer` (painted above `MapStaticCache`) only occludes moving light dots against `layout.boxes.values.map((r) => r.inflate(radius))` and not against `layout.cards`. On any active line (`animate != 'off'`), the animated light dots continue travelling across bordered category cards (through card headers, gaps between boxes in an intermediate column, and the 12 px margin between a card's border and its box) where the line itself was clipped away. Reproduce: turn on **Borders around category cards** while an agent is working and has an active line (e.g. a `default` model line crossing the Providers card).
5. **Medium** `flutter_app/lib/map_layout.dart:111`, `flutter_app/lib/map_layout.dart:373-395`, `flutter_app/lib/map_editor.dart:1264-1288`, `flutter_app/lib/map_editor.dart:1523-1569` With `card_border['on'] == true`, lines stop at the card border, but `MapCurve.hook` (`point(.91)`) and `layout.lineAt` still use the unclipped curve down to the box edge (`card.rect.left + 12` / `card.rect.right - 12`). Whenever the horizontal span between connected boxes is under `97` px (`columnGap < 73` px, including the default 4-column `columnGap = 36` px where `hook.dx = card.rect.left + 4.6`), the orange connection hook (`flutter_app/lib/map_editor.dart:1530-1569`) renders inside the target card's border past the clipped end of the line; and clicking empty space inside an intermediate bordered card (between boxes or in the 12 px padding) along a clipped line's path still hits `layout.lineAt` and selects the hidden line or opens its `failure`/`fixed` dialog. Reproduce: turn on **Borders around category cards**, select a connected box in the 4-column map to reveal the orange hook inside the neighbour card's border, or click the gap between two provider boxes in a card crossed by an agent-to-model line.
6. **Medium** `flutter_app/lib/map_layout.dart:491-504` When `used_only` is active on a card, `layout.cards[card]!.nodes` holds only the visible connected/pinned subset (`flutter_app/lib/map_layout.dart:232-234`). `reorderMapBox` writes `saved['order'][card] = ids` from `layout.cards[card]!.nodes`, dropping every hidden box in that card from `saved['order'][card]` and losing their custom order when `Show all` is restored. Reproduce: reorder unused boxes in a card, turn on `Show only what is connected or pinned` for that card, drag a visible box's reorder handle (or choose **Move up** / **Move down**), and click `Show all`.
7. **Low** `flutter_app/lib/settings_view.dart:220-228` In `_colorChoices`, `Tooltip.message` resolves `'theme:primary'`, `'theme:secondary'`, `'theme:tertiary'`, and `'theme:outline'` through `tr(_themeNames[value.substring(6)]!)`, but `Semantics.label` is set to raw `value` (`'theme:primary'`, etc.), exposing untranslated internal keys to assistive technologies. Reproduce: open **Settings › Map**, enable **Borders around category cards** (or inspect **Line colours**), and inspect the semantics label of any of the four theme colour buttons.

### Answers (Claude)
1. Fixed: a selection counts and lights only boxes on screen, so a box a card leaves out ends the dimming.
2. Fixed: an empty card is as tall as a folded one.
3. Fixed: lines between two agents are drawn after the clip, whole.
4. Fixed: over a bordered card the lights are hidden too.
5. Fixed: with borders, `hookOf` puts the hook where the line shows, and `lineAt` takes no click inside a card.
6. Fixed: the card keeps every box in `all`; a move fills the shown places only.
7. Fixed: the label is the translated name.

## Round 2 (Antigravity, 2026-11-02, commit 19c6e1e)

1. **Low** `flutter_app/lib/map_lights.dart:121-125`, `flutter_app/lib/map_lights.dart:145`, `flutter_app/lib/map_lights.dart:185` When one agent uses another while working (`edge['kind'] == agentLink`, `edge['active'] == true`), `_MapPaint` draws the `agent-link` bend whole over the Agents card even when `layout.bordered` is `true` (`flutter_app/lib/map_editor.dart:1872-1874`), and `hookOf` likewise exempts `agentLink` (`flutter_app/lib/map_layout.dart:392`). However, `MapLightTiles` applies card-rect occlusion (`layout.cards.values.map((c) => c.rect)`) to every curve including `agentLink`, hiding the moving light dots on the parts of the `agent-link` curve inside the Agents card (`t < 0.24` and `t > 0.76`); and `_makeTiles()` computes `Rect.fromPoints(curve.start, curve.end).inflate(radius)` (`bounds.right = a.right + 4`), which omits `curve.middle` (`a.right + 22`) so no light tile is created for the rightward bend when `a.right + 4` and `a.right + 16.5` fall in different 64 px tiles (e.g. `viewportWidth: 1600`, where `a.right = 762` is in tile `x = 11` and the bend at `x = 778.5` is in tile `x = 12`). Reproduce: turn on **Borders around category cards** (or resize the map to `viewportWidth: 1600` with no line crossing tile `x = 12` at the agents' Y range) while an agent is working and using another agent (`activity.<agent>.uses`).

### Answers (Claude)
1. Fixed: cards hide the lights of every line but the bend between two agents, and a tile covers a curve's whole path.

Maprisma now installs on a fresh Linux desktop from this page, without the AUR: unpack `maprisma-0.14.6-x86_64.tar.gz` and run `./install.sh` (for you, no root) or `sudo ./install.sh --system` (for every user). It checks the system first and changes nothing when something is missing, naming the packages for your distribution (Arch, Debian/Ubuntu/Pop!_OS, Fedora/Rocky, openSUSE); it never replaces another install's files; an update is all or nothing, and an interrupted one is rolled back by the next run; `uninstall.sh` removes only what it installed. The AppImage keeps working without installing.

Runs on glibc 2.34 and GLib 2.68 or newer (the release is built for them now). Tested on fresh Arch installs of COSMIC, KDE Plasma and Hyprland (with and without uwsm) and on Rocky Linux 9.

Maprisma Control (the bar icon, its own release 0.2.1) finds Maprisma installed from the archive, in /opt or as an AppImage.

**Files:** `maprisma-0.14.6-x86_64.tar.gz` (recommended), `Maprisma-0.14.6-x86_64.AppImage`, each with a `.sha256` (`sha256sum -c <file>.sha256`).
